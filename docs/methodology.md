# Descriptive policy and assumed stress responses

Financial information cutoff: 2026-10-21. Market bars end 2026-10-20. Fetch timestamps in the inventory may be October 22 UTC (October 21 Detroit); they are not observation dates.

`history.load()` extracts each rate from a frozen filing phrase and preserves the source URL. Seventeen announcements include the initial issue, a transition carry, and a contingent daily declaration. Only fourteen unconditional decisions enter the fit: eleven before the July 30 policy statement and three after it. The hold baseline exactly matches the three held rates; this is a tiny sample, not evidence of forecasting power.

`fit.evaluate()` fits eight candidate descriptive policies. It reports training error, leave-one-out diagnostics, a chronological holdout and an expanding replay starting after six decisions. Leave-one-out includes later training observations and is not a temporal forecast. The split and candidate models were selected after observing history. Predictions are rounded to a 25 basis-point grid; no model is selected as a proven controller. An intercept-plus-gap threshold fit can look good in sample without surviving a policy change.

Price features are volume-weighted daily typical-price proxies, not transaction VWAP. Month means calendar month through notice; early notices cannot use unseen month-end prices. Five-day windows exclude the final available monthly bar. Daily closes may occur after intraday announcements. BTC returns and the hindsight-scaled MSTR/BTC mNAV proxy are diagnostic covariates, not clean information available to management at the decision instant. Historical mNAV and the liabilities/BTC ratio are not reconstructed point in time. These limits also apply to holdout/replay interpretation.

Binding rate restrictions and discretionary guidance are separate. The filed reduction floor combines the prior rate, a 25 basis-point allowance, the lookback decline in Monthly SOFR, and the pre-notice Monthly SOFR floor. Unpaid accumulated dividends prevent a reduction. Increases have no encoded contractual ceiling. The implementation uses NY Fed overnight SOFR, not licensed CME one-month term SOFR: it cannot certify actual contractual compliance.

Stress outputs assume perpetual pricing, fixed notional/holdings/liabilities, a BTC-driven spread, flat SOFR unless specified, and a $101 price ceiling. Optional redemption does not guarantee a market-price ceiling. The two fitted spread forms are correlations, not causal estimates. BTC -40% sensitivity multiplies their slopes by 0.5, 1 and 1.5 and reanchors their initial yield. Future rate decisions remain discretionary. The `oracle` knows required yield and is only an idealized benchmark. Saturation means failure to regain the model's $99 band within 24 months, not default. The log spread form does not enforce an insolvency boundary; the distance form diverges at its assumed coverage point. Neither is a default model. Monthly simulation does not model the full semi-monthly payment calendar or legal notice process.
